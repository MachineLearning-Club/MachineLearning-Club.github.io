1. Which algorithm fits the question?

Real-life question. We want to predict a known category, or group players into unknown types.

ID3 and KNN are supervised classification; K-Means finds clusters without labels. The input and expected output determine the choice.

Worked example.

Known labels available + predict a new player → KNN or ID3. No labels + discover groups → K-Means.

print({'ID3':'learn rules', 'KNN':'vote', 'K-Means':'group'})
Show the process step by step
  1. Identify labelled examples
  2. Define the desired output
  3. Select the matching method

2. K-nearest neighbours

Real-life question. A new runner has measurements similar to several known runners.

KNN predicts the most common label among the K closest training examples. Unlike K-Means, it requires labels.

Worked example.

For nearest labels ['fast','fast','slow'] and K=3, prediction='fast'.

labels = ['fast','fast','slow']
print(max(set(labels),key=labels.count))
Show the process step by step
  1. Compute distances
  2. Take the K closest
  3. Count their labels
