Perceptrons: A First Learning Neuron

Understand weights, bias and a decision boundary before a large neural network.

A closer look at the algorithm

A perceptron multiplies inputs by weights, adds a bias, then applies a simple activation rule.

Worked reasoning

For x=(2,1), w=(1,-1), bias=-0.5, weighted total=2-1-0.5=0.5.

One worked algorithmic trace
  1. Multiply each input by its weight
  2. Add the bias
  3. Apply the step rule
