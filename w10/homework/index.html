Week 10 · Homework

Generative AI: GPT, Data and Society

Tokens, next-word prediction, social-media data, ethics and responsible use.

Real-life situation

A school's media team is considering using an AI assistant to write newsletter summaries.

Mandatory (1p)

Tokenise a short sentence and identify two risks of trusting its generated completion.

Suggested starting steps
  1. Open the Week 10 Laboratory and run the prepared code.
  2. Identify the relevant input and output.
  3. Change the smallest amount of code needed to solve the situation.

Homework (2p)

Build a table of three generated claims, their source evidence and a verdict.

What to submit

Use the laboratory submission area to prepare a named notebook or Python file. Add one screenshot, output table or short explanation of what changed.
