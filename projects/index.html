Competition preparation

  1. Week 12: read a practice specification, inspect the dataset, and establish a reproducible baseline.
  2. Week 13: implement a complete application and confirm its input-output contract.
  3. Week 14: compare models on validation data, report mistakes, and freeze the chosen configuration.
  4. Week 15: perform one final evaluation, test the project, and prepare an independent demonstration.
  5. When the organisers publish actual FiiCode requirements, review the official rules and adapt the project rather than assuming the practice task matches them.

The Study Space Challenge is an independent classroom exercise; no competition date, platform or assessment rule is asserted here.
