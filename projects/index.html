Competition preparation

  1. February: introduce FIICode, recruit interested students and verify official registration announcements.
  2. March: respond to an official challenge if published; otherwise practise with a representative dataset.
  3. 24 March: establish an evaluation metric and baseline.
  4. 31 March: improve one component and analyse errors.
  5. 7 April: present and test a complete project.
  6. April–May: optional additional mentoring if any students qualify for a final.

The official FIICode 2027 schedule is not yet confirmed; these are preparation milestones, not announced contest dates.
