Week 05 · Homework

Computer Vision: Images as Data

Situation — recover a faint worksheet

A teacher has a low-contrast synthetic worksheet represented as a table of pixel intensities. Your task is to separate dark writing from pale paper, show every numerical decision, and explain the cost of changing the image.

Mandatory (1p) — process six pixels by hand

  1. Apply threshold T=128 to [30,100,127,128,220,250] with the convention v<T→0 and v≥T→255.
  2. Repeat the calculation with T=200. How many pixels change output?
  3. For RGB=(120,180,240), compute the arithmetic mean and the weighted grayscale value 0.2126R+0.7152G+0.0722B.
  4. Using patch [[0,0,0],[0,255,255],[0,255,255]], calculate the 3×3 mean.
Check only after attempting

T=128: [0,0,0,255,255,255]. T=200: [0,0,0,0,255,255], so one output changes. RGB mean 180; weighted 171.576 (about 172); 3×3 mean 1020/9≈113.33.

Homework (2p) — evaluate three processing choices

  1. Open the prepared image notebook and plot its original 5×5 numerical image.
  2. Compare thresholding at 55, 128 and 200. For each record the number of black pixels and include the resulting image or matrix.
  3. Change one bright-background pixel to intensity 0 to simulate a noisy sensor. Show the original and altered array.
  4. Try the provided 3×3 smoothing function. Explain whether it removes the artificial speck, and whether it blurs nearby strokes.
  5. Write a five-sentence recommendation for improving worksheet readability without promising perfect optical character recognition.

Submission format: one notebook with three images or text grids, a small comparison table and your explanation. All images are synthetic; no student photographs are needed.

Research (3p) — optional

Implement the Vision Lab 1 kernel [[0,-2,0],[-2,8,-2],[0,-2,0]] on the central 3×3 patch. Show the nine products and sum. State whether you used clipping to 0–255; discuss one situation where sharpening amplifies noise.

What to submit

Use the Week 5 laboratory submission area to prepare a named ZIP of the notebook and your calculations. This does not transmit files to the teacher automatically.
