Syllabus

Explain it. Calculate it. Test it. Build it.

Every week includes learning outcomes in four parts: Course, Laboratory, Homework and optional Advanced.

Learning progression

By Week 3

Use probability and information gain to draw an ID3 tree.

By Week 6

Evaluate classifiers and apply KNN and K-Means by hand.

By Week 9

Fit a regression, work with image filters and evaluate a perceptron.

By Week 12

Describe and test a multicomponent system and verify claims from an LLM.

By Week 15

Demonstrate a reproducible project with a metric and clear limitations.

Method

  • 5 min recap and motivation
  • 15 min conceptual explanation
  • 15 min worked exercise on paper
  • 20 min guided PRIMM coding laboratory
  • 5 min exit ticket or reflection

Weekly course objectives

  1. Week 1: Distinguish programmed rules from machine learning
  2. Week 2: Calculate means medians and frequencies
  3. Week 3: Identify nodes branches and leaves
  4. Week 4: Choose numeric thresholds
  5. Week 5: Compute Euclidean distance in 2D
  6. Week 6: Distinguish classification from clustering
  7. Week 7: Interpret slope and intercept
  8. Week 8: Read grayscale pixel matrices
  9. Week 9: Compute a weighted sum and bias
  10. Week 10: Draw stages of an AI pipeline
  11. Week 11: Separate data cleaning from modelling
  12. Week 12: Explain next-token prediction
  13. Week 13: Identify the target and evaluation metric
  14. Week 14: Group important model errors
  15. Week 15: Explain the full pipeline
