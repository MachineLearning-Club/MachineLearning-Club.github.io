Compare squared distances before and after the update

Before the first update, the sum of the squared distances to the assigned initial centres is 7.

After the update, the squared distances are 10/9, 10/9, 4/9, 1/4, 1/4.

Sum: 24/9 + 1/2 = 8/3 + 1/2 = 19/6 ≈ 3.167.

Explain: why is a smaller number desirable for this objective?

Conclusion: does the algorithm promise the best possible clustering from any starting points?

Expected reasoning

No. K-means repeatedly improves its current assignment and centroids, but the final outcome can depend on the initial centres. Its objective value is not a certificate of the only meaningful real-world grouping.
