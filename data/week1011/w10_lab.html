4. Check a generated newsletter sentence

An imagined draft claims “80% of pupils voted yes.” The fictional tally is 12 yes and 8 no. Work out the true percentage and record claim / evidence / verdict. This is verification—not a contest to make the AI sound convincing.

Reveal the check

12÷(12+8)=12÷20=60%. The claimed 80% does not match the data. The newsletter must be corrected before publication.

Research (3p) — optional
