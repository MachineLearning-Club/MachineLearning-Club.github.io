FIICode: Baselines and Metrics

Turn a competition task into reproducible measurable experiments.

CourseLaboratoryHomeworkAdvanced

Laboratory objectives

  • Inspect a dataset
  • Train a first baseline
  • Record a reproducible score

Lab activities

  1. Use the official challenge when available or a practice dataset
  2. Create a data dictionary and evaluation strategy
  3. Fit a baseline and save parameters and score

What to record

Record a worked example, a tested result and one limitation. Use PRIMM: Predict, Run, Investigate, Modify, Make.
