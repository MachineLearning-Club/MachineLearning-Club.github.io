Week 09 · Homework

Mid-Term Evaluation

Demonstrate your understanding through practical tasks and short explanations.

Real-life situation

A science-fair panel asks for proof that a student's model works on unfamiliar inputs.

Mandatory (1p)

Prepare a one-page visual explanation of a model.

Suggested starting steps
  1. Open the Week 9 Laboratory and run the prepared code.
  2. Identify the relevant input and output.
  3. Change the smallest amount of code needed to solve the situation.

Homework (2p)

Correct a small notebook and add an honest evaluation table.

What to submit

Use the laboratory submission area to prepare a named notebook or Python file. Add one screenshot, output table or short explanation of what changed.
