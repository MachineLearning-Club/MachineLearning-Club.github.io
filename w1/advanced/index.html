Week 01 · Advanced

What Is AI? Rules and Learning

Rules, search, learning from examples and language.

Reasoning exercises

This sheet develops a single idea through small, related problems: different models can agree on the training examples and disagree on an unseen example. It uses truth tables, ordinary pseudocode and counterexamples; there is no formal verification software or installation.

Read the A4 exercise sheet (PDF) · LaTeX source · Accessible print version.

The PDF contains one complete worked example, four progressive problems, hints and a selected worked solution. All exercises can be solved with a pencil and a four-row table.

How this connects to the course

The Laboratory asks why a learned prediction may fail. The Advanced sheet asks a more precise question: can the supplied examples logically rule out a competing model? The answer uses a concrete counterexample, not abstract proof machinery.

Further reading: Experience AI, Bias in, bias out · IOAI classical machine-learning foundations.
